About MARK Expense Tracker

MARK Expense Tracker is a comprehensive budgeting and expense management tool designed to help you take control of your finances.

  • Set and monitor your monthly budget.
  • Get visual insights with charts and reports.
  • Analyze spending patterns with summary and reports pages.
  • Filter expenses by category to see where your money goes.
  • Receive alerts if you exceed your budget.
